## English

JUMPLINE GO ("the game") does not collect personal data.

- **Stored information:** Your progress (unlocked levels, stars, gold/gems, costumes, settings, task and
  achievement counters) is stored only on your device in the game's local storage. It is never sent to any server
  and cannot be seen by the developer.
- **Internet:** The game works offline. It contains no analytics, tracking or advertising libraries.
- **Permissions:** No special permission is used except vibration.
- **Children:** The game does not knowingly collect data from children; it collects no data from anyone.
- **Deletion:** Uninstalling the game or clearing its app data removes all progress from your device.
